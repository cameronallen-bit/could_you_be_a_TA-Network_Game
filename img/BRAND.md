## Type

- Family: **Barlow** (Google Fonts), with `sans-serif` as the fallback. The page loads weights 400, 500, 600 and 700. Icons are Font Awesome.
- **H1 (hero):** Barlow 400, 50 px / 55 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.8 px, white.
- **H2 / H3:** Barlow 400, 30–32 px / about 42–45 px, mostly uppercase, `#3C4B55` (white on dark bands).
- **Hero subhead:** Barlow 400, 30 px, white, sentence case.
- **Body:** Barlow 400. The base size is 16 px; content paragraphs are 18 px / 28 px in `#3C4B55`. Bold lead-ins are 700.
- **Links:** Barlow 500, `#5191AB`, no underline until hover.
- **Nav:** Barlow 500, 14 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.8 px, white.
- **Footer bottom bar:** Barlow 400, 12.8 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.8 px.
