## Notes

- **01:** Unsplash places this photo at Nassau, The Bahamas. It reads as Caribbean, and no land is distinctive enough to name.
- **02:** Unsplash places this photo near Athens, Greece. The sea in it is plain open water, so it reads as "at sea" anywhere. The faces are not visible. Unsplash photos come with no model release; with the faces hidden, the risk is low.
- **03:** The Commons description reads "Divi tree, Aruba" and doesn't name the beach. Eagle Beach is known for these trees, but the location isn't confirmed. The photo is not Curaçao. It has strong HDR-style processing.
- **04:** A cruise line logo is visible on the funnel. It appears to be Princess Cruises' sea witch, but I haven't confirmed that. That's fine for the quiz. For an ad that isn't about that line, swap the photo or retouch the logo.
- **05:** The people form a group of adult passengers, not clearly a family. Everyone is seen from behind; no model release. A closer family match is listed under "Gaps" below.
- **06:** This is a U.S. government work, so no permission is needed. Don't imply USDA endorsement.
- **07:** AmaWaterways' own media library (Acquia DAM, linked from amawaterways.com) has better shots, such as "AmaViola_exterior_AT_Danube.jpg". Downloading from it requires accepting an end-user license agreement that limits use to registered dealers and media, so I declined it rather than accept it for you. See "Gaps" below.
- **10:** The NCLH press kit has no usage statement. For paid ads, confirm with Regent PR (PublicRelations@rssc.com, North America via Quinn PR, RSSC@quinn.pr) or use Regent's travel-partner marketing assets.
